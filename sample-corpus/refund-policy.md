# Refund and Returns Policy

## Eligibility

Customers may request a refund under the following conditions:

1. **Software subscriptions**: Full refund within 14 days of purchase if no more than 100 API calls have been made. After 14 days, refunds are prorated based on remaining term.

2. **Professional services**: Refund available before work commences. Once a statement of work is signed and engagement begins, only unused prepaid hours are refundable.

3. **Training and certifications**: Full refund if cancelled 7+ days before the scheduled date. 50% refund if cancelled 2-6 days before. No refund for cancellations within 48 hours.

## Refund Process

1. Submit refund request via support.acme.example/refunds
2. Include order number, reason for refund, and preferred refund method
3. Team reviews within 3 business days
4. Approved refunds processed within 5-7 business days
5. Refund issued to original payment method unless otherwise requested

## Non-Refundable Items

- Custom development work delivered and accepted
- Data migration services (once migration is complete)
- Domain registration fees
- Third-party license pass-throughs
- Setup fees after environment provisioning

## Disputes

If a refund request is denied, customers may:
1. Request manager review within 14 days of denial
2. Escalate to the Customer Ombudsman (ombudsman@acme.example)
3. Final escalation to binding arbitration per the master agreement

## Credit Notes

In lieu of a cash refund, customers may opt for account credit at 110% of the refund value. Credits are valid for 12 months and applicable to any Acme Corp product or service.
