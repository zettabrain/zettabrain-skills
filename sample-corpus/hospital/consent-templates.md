# Informed Consent Language Templates

## General Surgical Consent

The patient has been informed of and understands:

1. **Diagnosis**: The nature of the condition requiring treatment
2. **Proposed Procedure**: [PROCEDURE NAME] including technique and approach
3. **Risks**: Including but not limited to: bleeding, infection, adverse reaction to anesthesia, nerve damage, scarring, need for additional procedures, and in rare cases, death
4. **Benefits**: Expected outcomes and likelihood of success
5. **Alternatives**: Including non-surgical options, watchful waiting, and alternative procedures
6. **Recovery**: Expected timeline, limitations, follow-up requirements

The patient acknowledges that no guarantees have been made regarding outcomes.

## Anesthesia Consent

I consent to the administration of anesthesia as deemed appropriate by the anesthesiology team. I understand:

- Type of anesthesia planned: General / Regional / Sedation / Local
- Risks include: allergic reaction, nausea, sore throat, dental damage, awareness during procedure, respiratory complications, cardiac complications
- The anesthesiologist may modify the anesthetic technique if clinically necessary
- I have disclosed all medications, supplements, and recreational substances
- I have followed NPO (nothing by mouth) instructions as directed

## Blood Transfusion Consent

I consent to receive blood products (packed red blood cells, platelets, plasma, cryoprecipitate) if deemed medically necessary. I understand:

- Blood is screened but carries a small risk of: infection (HIV, Hepatitis B/C), allergic reaction, transfusion reaction, iron overload
- Alternatives discussed: autologous donation, cell salvage, volume expanders
- I may withdraw consent at any time, understanding the medical risks of refusal
- Religious/personal objections have been documented: YES / NO

## Telemedicine Consent

I consent to receive healthcare services via telemedicine technology. I understand:

- The consultation occurs via secure, HIPAA-compliant video/audio connection
- The provider may not be able to perform a physical examination
- Technical difficulties may interrupt the session
- I am responsible for ensuring a private location during the consultation
- Emergency services: if a medical emergency arises, I will call 911 or proceed to the nearest ED
- The same standard of care applies as in-person visits
- My insurance may or may not cover telemedicine visits

## Research/Clinical Trial Consent

Participation in this research study is entirely voluntary. I understand:

- Purpose of the study: [STUDY DESCRIPTION]
- Duration of participation: [TIMEFRAME]
- Procedures involved: [PROCEDURES]
- Potential risks: [RISKS]
- Potential benefits: direct benefit is not guaranteed
- Compensation: [COMPENSATION DETAILS]
- Confidentiality: data will be de-identified; results may be published
- Right to withdraw at any time without penalty or loss of standard care
- Contact: Principal Investigator [NAME] at [PHONE] for questions
- IRB contact: [IRB PHONE] for concerns about rights as a research subject

## Right to Refuse

The patient has the right to refuse any proposed treatment or procedure. If the patient refuses, the following has been documented:

1. The risks of refusing treatment have been explained in plain language
2. The patient demonstrates understanding of potential consequences
3. Alternative options have been offered
4. The patient's decision is voluntary and not made under duress
5. The patient has been informed they may change their decision at any time
