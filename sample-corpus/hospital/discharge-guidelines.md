# Discharge Documentation Guidelines

## Required Elements for All Discharges

Every discharge summary must contain:

1. **Patient Demographics**: Name, MRN, DOB, admission/discharge dates
2. **Admitting Diagnosis**: ICD-10 code and plain language description
3. **Discharge Diagnosis**: Final diagnosis (may differ from admitting)
4. **Hospital Course**: Brief summary of treatments, procedures, and patient response
5. **Discharge Condition**: Stable, improved, unchanged, or AMA
6. **Discharge Medications**: Complete list with dosage, frequency, duration, and changes from admission
7. **Follow-up Plan**: Provider, timeframe, and purpose of each follow-up
8. **Patient Education**: Topics covered, understanding verified, materials provided
9. **Activity Restrictions**: Specific limitations and duration
10. **Return Precautions**: Clear "return to ED if..." criteria in patient-friendly language

## Medication Reconciliation Requirements

- Compare admission medication list with discharge medications
- Document rationale for any changes (new, modified, discontinued)
- Flag high-risk medications: anticoagulants, insulin, opioids, immunosuppressants
- Verify patient/caregiver can state: drug name, purpose, dose, frequency, side effects
- Ensure 30-day supply prescribed or confirmed with pharmacy

## Condition-Specific Discharge Requirements

### Heart Failure
- Daily weight instructions (report 2+ lb gain in 24h or 5+ lb in 1 week)
- Fluid restriction specified (typically 1.5-2L/day)
- Low-sodium diet counseling (<2000mg/day)
- Cardiology follow-up within 7 days
- BNP/proBNP at follow-up for comparison

### Post-Surgical
- Wound care instructions with visual aids
- Signs of infection to watch for
- Pain management plan (multimodal, opioid taper schedule if applicable)
- Surgeon follow-up within 10-14 days
- Physical therapy referral if indicated

### Psychiatric
- Safety plan documented and copy provided to patient
- 72-hour follow-up appointment confirmed (not just scheduled)
- Crisis hotline numbers provided (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
- Medication adherence plan with identified barriers
- Support system contacts notified (with patient consent)
- Lethal means restriction counseling documented

## Timing Requirements

- Discharge summary completed within 24 hours of discharge
- Transmitted to PCP within 48 hours
- Pending results flagged with responsible provider assigned
- Patient portal access confirmed for accessing their records

## Readmission Prevention Checklist

Before discharge, verify:
- [ ] Patient verbalizes understanding of diagnosis
- [ ] Teach-back completed for all new medications
- [ ] Follow-up appointments scheduled (not "call to schedule")
- [ ] Transportation to follow-up confirmed
- [ ] Home support assessed (can patient manage ADLs independently?)
- [ ] Pharmacy confirmed medication availability and insurance coverage
- [ ] Red flag symptoms explained in language patient understands
- [ ] Contact number provided for questions post-discharge
