# Legal Fee Schedule

## Hourly Rates

| Attorney Level | Rate |
|----------------|------|
| Senior Partner | $650/hour |
| Junior Partner | $475/hour |
| Senior Associate | $375/hour |
| Associate | $275/hour |
| Paralegal | $150/hour |
| Legal Assistant | $95/hour |

## Fixed Fee Services

### Business Formation
- LLC Formation (single state): $1,500
- Corporation Formation (single state): $2,200
- Partnership Agreement: $1,800
- Operating Agreement: $1,200

### Real Estate
- Residential Closing (buyer): $1,200
- Residential Closing (seller): $900
- Commercial Lease Review: $2,500
- Title Search and Opinion: $750

### Estate Planning
- Simple Will: $500
- Trust Creation (Revocable Living Trust): $2,500
- Power of Attorney: $350
- Healthcare Directive: $300
- Complete Estate Package (will + trust + POA + directive): $3,200

## Retainer Requirements

- Litigation matters: $10,000 minimum retainer
- Corporate advisory: $5,000 monthly retainer
- Ongoing consultation: $2,500 quarterly retainer
- Retainer replenishment required when balance falls below 25%

## Billing Terms

- Invoiced monthly on the 1st
- Payment due within 30 days (Net-30)
- Late payment fee: 1.5% per month on balances over 30 days
- Minimum billing increment: 6 minutes (0.1 hour)
- Travel time billed at 50% of attorney's hourly rate
- Court filing fees, courier services, and expert witness fees billed at cost

## Contingency Fee Arrangements

Available for personal injury and employment discrimination cases only:
- 33.3% of recovery if settled before filing
- 40% of recovery if settled after filing but before trial
- 45% of recovery if case proceeds to trial
- Client responsible for court costs and expenses regardless of outcome
