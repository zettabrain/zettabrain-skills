# Employee Leave Policy

## 1. Annual Leave Entitlement

All full-time employees are entitled to the following paid leave:

- **0-2 years service**: 15 working days per year
- **2-5 years service**: 20 working days per year
- **5+ years service**: 25 working days per year

Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis calculated against their contracted hours.

## 2. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to up to 10 paid sick days per calendar year. A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days. Unused sick leave does not carry over.

## 3. Parental Leave

- **Primary caregiver**: 16 weeks paid leave at full salary
- **Secondary caregiver**: 4 weeks paid leave at full salary
- Leave must commence within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption date

## 4. Bereavement Leave

- Immediate family (spouse, children, parents): 5 working days
- Extended family (siblings, grandparents): 3 working days
- Close friend or colleague: 1 working day

## 5. Public Holidays

The company observes 10 public holidays per year as designated by the regional office. When a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday is observed.

## 6. Leave Request Process

1. Submit leave request via the HR portal at least 14 days in advance
2. Manager approval required within 3 business days
3. Emergency leave: notify manager within 2 hours of shift start
4. Requests during blackout periods (Q4 close, product launches) require director approval

## 7. Carryover Policy

A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried days must be used by March 31. No cash equivalent is provided for unused leave.
