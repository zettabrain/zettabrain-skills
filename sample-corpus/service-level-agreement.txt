SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT - CLOUD PLATFORM SERVICES

1. SERVICE AVAILABILITY

1.1 Acme Corp guarantees 99.95% monthly uptime for all production services measured over each calendar month.

1.2 Scheduled maintenance windows (Tuesday/Thursday 02:00-04:00 UTC) are excluded from availability calculations.

1.3 Uptime is calculated as: (Total Minutes - Downtime Minutes) / Total Minutes * 100

2. RESPONSE TIME TARGETS

Severity 1 (Service Down): Response within 15 minutes, resolution target 4 hours
Severity 2 (Major Impact): Response within 30 minutes, resolution target 8 hours
Severity 3 (Minor Impact): Response within 2 hours, resolution target 24 hours
Severity 4 (Low Priority): Response within 8 hours, resolution target 5 business days

3. SERVICE CREDITS

If monthly uptime falls below guaranteed levels, customers receive:
- 99.0% - 99.95%: 10% credit on monthly invoice
- 95.0% - 99.0%: 25% credit on monthly invoice
- Below 95.0%: 50% credit on monthly invoice

Credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident. Maximum credit per month: 50% of monthly fee.

4. EXCLUSIONS

The SLA does not apply to:
- Force majeure events
- Customer-caused outages (misconfiguration, overload)
- Scheduled maintenance within designated windows
- Beta or pre-release features
- Services suspended due to non-payment

5. MONITORING AND REPORTING

5.1 Real-time status page: status.acme.example
5.2 Monthly uptime reports delivered by the 5th business day
5.3 Post-incident reports within 48 hours for Severity 1/2 events

6. SUPPORT CHANNELS

- Web portal: support.acme.example (24/7)
- Phone: +1-800-ACME-SOS (Severity 1/2 only, 24/7)
- Email: support@acme.example (response within 4 hours during business hours)
- Dedicated Slack channel for Enterprise customers
