# Site Safety Requirements

## OSHA Compliance Minimums

All Titan Construction Group projects must meet or exceed OSHA 29 CFR 1926 standards for construction.

### Required Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

All personnel on active construction sites must wear:
- Hard hat (ANSI Z89.1 Type I or Type II) at all times
- Safety glasses (ANSI Z87.1) at all times
- High-visibility vest (ANSI 107 Class 2 minimum)
- Steel-toed boots (ASTM F2413)
- Hearing protection when noise exceeds 85 dBA
- Fall protection when working at heights above 6 feet

### Fall Protection Requirements

- Guardrails: top rail at 42" (+/- 3"), mid-rail at 21", toe board at 4"
- Personal fall arrest systems: maximum free fall distance of 6 feet
- Safety nets: installed within 30 feet below work surface
- Leading edge work: 6-foot trigger height, full-body harness required
- Roof work: warning line at 6 feet from edge, or personal fall arrest
- Ladders: secured at top, extend 3 feet above landing, 4:1 ratio

### Excavation and Trenching

- Competent person must inspect daily and after rain events
- Trenches 5+ feet deep require protective system (sloping, shoring, or shield)
- Trenches 20+ feet deep require engineered protective system
- Spoil pile minimum 2 feet from edge of excavation
- Means of egress (ladder/ramp) within 25 feet of travel for trenches 4+ feet deep
- Utility locates required 48 hours before digging (call 811)

### Electrical Safety

- GFCI protection on all 120V, 15/20A receptacles on construction sites
- Lockout/tagout procedures for all electrical work
- Minimum approach distances: 10 feet for lines up to 50kV
- Only qualified electricians may work on energized circuits
- Temporary wiring must be protected from damage and weather

## Titan-Specific Requirements (Above OSHA Minimums)

- Daily safety briefing (toolbox talk) before work commences
- 100% tie-off policy at any height (not just above 6 feet)
- Drug and alcohol testing: pre-employment, random, post-incident
- Incident reporting within 1 hour to Safety Department
- Near-miss reporting encouraged (no-fault policy)
- Weekly site safety audit by superintendent
- Monthly third-party safety inspection on projects > $1M

## Subcontractor Requirements

All subcontractors must provide:
1. Certificate of insurance meeting Titan's minimum requirements ($2M general liability, $1M auto, workers' comp statutory limits)
2. Written safety program
3. OSHA 300 logs for previous 3 years (EMR must be below 1.0)
4. Proof of employee training (10-hour OSHA minimum, 30-hour for supervisors)
5. Competent person designation for applicable work (excavation, scaffolding, cranes)

## Incident Response

### Injury Protocol
1. Secure the scene and render first aid
2. Call 911 if life-threatening
3. Notify site superintendent immediately
4. Notify Safety Department within 1 hour
5. Preserve evidence and document conditions
6. Drug test injured worker and witnesses within 8 hours
7. Complete incident report within 24 hours

### OSHA Reporting Triggers
- Fatality: report within 8 hours
- Inpatient hospitalization: report within 24 hours
- Amputation: report within 24 hours
- Loss of eye: report within 24 hours
