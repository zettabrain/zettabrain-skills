# Standard Bid Terms and Conditions

## Bid Validity

All bids/proposals submitted by Titan Construction Group are valid for 30 calendar days from the date of submission unless otherwise stated. After 30 days, pricing is subject to revision due to material cost fluctuations and labor availability.

## Inclusions (Unless Specifically Excluded)

Standard bids include:
- All labor, materials, and equipment for the described scope
- Project management and site supervision
- Standard cleanup and debris removal
- As-built documentation
- One-year workmanship warranty from date of substantial completion
- General liability and workers' compensation insurance
- Performance and payment bonds (if required and specified)

## Standard Exclusions

Unless explicitly stated in the scope of work, bids exclude:
- Architectural and engineering design services
- Permit fees and inspection fees
- Utility connection fees and tap charges
- Hazardous material testing, abatement, or disposal
- Soil testing and geotechnical engineering
- Survey and staking
- Winter conditions protection (heating, hoarding, snow removal)
- Premium time (overtime, weekend, holiday work)
- Temporary power and water beyond standard site trailer
- Furniture, fixtures, and equipment (FF&E)
- Technology/low voltage (data, security, AV)
- Landscaping and irrigation
- Rock excavation or dewatering
- Concealed conditions discovered during construction

## Change Order Policy

- All changes must be documented in a written Change Order before work begins
- Change Orders include: description of change, cost impact, schedule impact
- Mark-up on Change Orders: 15% overhead and profit on self-performed work, 10% on subcontracted work
- Time and Material (T&M) work: labor at standard rates + 15%, materials at cost + 15%
- No verbal authorizations accepted
- Client has 5 business days to approve or reject a Change Order
- Disputed Change Orders: work proceeds if directed in writing; costs resolved per dispute clause

## Payment Terms

- Progress billing monthly based on percentage of completion
- Applications for payment submitted by the 25th of each month
- Payment due within 30 days of approved application (Net-30)
- Retainage: 10% withheld until substantial completion, reduced to 5% during warranty period
- Late payment: 1.5% interest per month on balances past due
- Mechanic's lien rights reserved per state statute
- Final payment due within 30 days of final completion and acceptance

## Schedule

- Construction schedule provided within 14 days of Notice to Proceed
- Updated monthly and submitted with payment applications
- Owner-caused delays: Titan entitled to time extension and delay damages
- Weather delays: time extension only (no additional cost) for days exceeding 10-year historical average
- Liquidated damages: only if specified in contract and mutually agreed upon

## Warranty

- Workmanship warranty: 1 year from substantial completion
- Material warranties: manufacturer's standard warranty (passed through to owner)
- Warranty excludes: normal wear and tear, owner modifications, improper maintenance, acts of God
- Warranty service response: within 48 hours for urgent items, 5 business days for routine

## Insurance Requirements

Titan Construction Group maintains:
- General Liability: $2,000,000 per occurrence / $5,000,000 aggregate
- Automobile Liability: $2,000,000 combined single limit
- Workers' Compensation: statutory limits
- Umbrella/Excess: $10,000,000
- Professional Liability (if design-build): $2,000,000
- Builder's Risk: project-specific, 100% of contract value

## Dispute Resolution

1. Good-faith negotiation between project principals (14 days)
2. Non-binding mediation (30 days)
3. Binding arbitration per AAA Construction Industry Rules
4. Prevailing party entitled to reasonable attorneys' fees and costs
