## Markup and Overhead

- General overhead: 12% of direct costs
- Profit margin: 8-15% depending on project complexity
- Bonds (performance + payment): 2.5% of contract value
- General liability insurance: 1.8% of contract value
- Builder's risk insurance: 0.5-1.0% of contract value
- Contingency (standard): 5% of estimated cost
- Contingency (renovation/unknown conditions): 10-15%
