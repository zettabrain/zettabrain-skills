## Products & Services

### Refrigerants

1. **R-410A Refrigerant**
   - Most common residential AC refrigerant
   - Available in 25 lb cylinders
   - Price: $85/lb
   - Use: Residential and light commercial AC systems

2. **R-22 Refrigerant (Freon)**
   - Legacy refrigerant for older systems
   - Limited supply, being phased out
   - Available in 30 lb cylinders
   - Price: $150/lb
   - Use: Older residential and commercial systems

3. **R-134a Refrigerant**
   - Automotive and commercial refrigeration
   - Available in 30 lb cylinders
   - Price: $45/lb
   - Use: Vehicle AC, commercial chillers

4. **R-404A Refrigerant**
   - Commercial and industrial refrigeration
   - Available in 24 lb cylinders
   - Price: $95/lb
   - Use: Walk-in coolers, freezers, ice machines

5. **R-407C Refrigerant**
   - R-22 replacement option
   - Available in 25 lb cylinders
   - Price: $70/lb
   - Use: Commercial AC retrofit systems
