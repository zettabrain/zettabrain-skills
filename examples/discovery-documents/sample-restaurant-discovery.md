# Sample Restaurant Discovery Document

## Company Information

**Company Name:** The Urban Plate  
**Industry:** Restaurant / Fine Dining  
**Description:** Contemporary farm-to-table restaurant specializing in seasonal American cuisine with global influences. Focus on locally-sourced ingredients and craft cocktails.

## Service Area

Dine-in service, takeout available, catering for events up to 100 people within 10-mile radius.

## Services

### Dining Services

1. **Lunch Service**
   - Available Tuesday - Friday
   - Hours: 11:30 AM - 2:30 PM
   - Average check: $25-35 per person
   - Reservations recommended

2. **Dinner Service**
   - Available Tuesday - Sunday
   - Hours: 5:30 PM - 10:00 PM
   - Average check: $55-75 per person
   - Reservations required on weekends

3. **Private Dining**
   - Private room seats up to 24 guests
   - Customizable prix fixe menus
   - Starting at $85 per person
   - $500 room fee waived for groups of 20+

4. **Catering Services**
   - Drop-off or full-service catering
   - Drop-off: Minimum $500 order
   - Full-service: Minimum $2000, includes staff
   - Service charge: 20% of food total

### Menu Categories

**Appetizers:** $12-18  
**Salads:** $14-16  
**Main Courses:** $28-45  
**Desserts:** $10-14  
**Craft Cocktails:** $14-16  
**Wine by Glass:** $12-22  

## Cuisine Specialties

- Seasonal rotating menu (changes quarterly)
- Locally-sourced produce from regional farms
- Sustainable seafood selections
- House-made pastas
- Wood-fired grill specialties
- Vegan and gluten-free options available

## Pricing Structure

- Food cost target: 30%
- Appetizers markup: 300%
- Entrees markup: 280%
- Desserts markup: 350%
- Beverages markup: 400%
- Catering markup: 250%

## Payment Terms

- Dine-in: Payment at end of meal
- Private dining: 50% deposit required, balance due day of event
- Catering: Full payment due 3 days before event
- Accepted: All major credit cards, cash
- 20% gratuity automatically added for parties of 6 or more

## Policies

**Reservation Policy:**
- Cancellations accepted up to 24 hours in advance
- No-show charge: $25 per person for parties of 6+
- Walk-ins accepted based on availability

**Dietary Accommodations:**
- Advance notice appreciated for allergies/restrictions
- Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free options available
- Cannot guarantee complete allergen-free environment

**Private Events:**
- Minimum 12 guests for private room
- 48-hour notice required for menu customization
- Bar minimum: $500 or à la carte

## Business Hours

- Tuesday - Thursday: Dinner only (5:30 PM - 10:00 PM)
- Friday: Lunch (11:30 AM - 2:30 PM) & Dinner (5:30 PM - 11:00 PM)
- Saturday: Dinner only (5:00 PM - 11:00 PM)
- Sunday: Brunch (10:00 AM - 2:00 PM) & Dinner (5:00 PM - 9:00 PM)
- Monday: Closed

- Urban professionals

- Special occasion diners
- Corporate events
