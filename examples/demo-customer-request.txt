## For the Demo with Mike, Use Scenario 1:

### Command to run:

```bash
zbs generate examples/3rva-quote-full.md \
  --input "Emergency request from Premier HVAC Services. Medical office building AC is down in Glen Allen VA. Need 100 lbs R-22 reclaimed refrigerant delivered same-day to 4500 Cox Road, Glen Allen VA 23060. Critical - patients on site. Need by 4pm today. Contact John Martinez at (804) 555-1234." \
  --output premier-hvac-emergency-quote.md
```

This will generate:
✅ Full itemized quote with pricing
✅ Volume discount (5% for 100 lbs)
✅ Same-day emergency delivery fee
✅ Glen Allen Zone 2 delivery
✅ Cylinder deposits
✅ Tax calculation
✅ Professional format ready to send

### Expected Total:
- Product: 100 lbs × $65/lb = $6,500
- Volume discount: -$325 (5%)
- Product subtotal: $6,175
- Cylinders: $400 deposit (refundable)
- Delivery Zone 2: $45
- Same-day emergency: $75
- Environmental fee: $5
- Subtotal: $6,700
- Tax (5.3%): $355.10
- **TOTAL: $7,055.10** (includes $400 refundable deposit)
- **Net payment: $6,655.10**

This shows the full power of the system - it reads the pricing rules, calculates everything correctly, and generates a professional quote ready to send to the customer.
